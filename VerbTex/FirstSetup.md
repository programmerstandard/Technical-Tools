# VerbTeX LaTeX Editor by verbosus.com First Setup

1. Download and install from Google 
's Android Play Store.
2. Open VerbTex LaTeX Editor by verbosus.com app.
3. Read and understood the Terms And Conditions. Then Tap Accept button to accept the Terms And Conditions.

## Link To Dropbox Cloud Drive

1. Tap APP on the top right corner. 
2. Tap Link to Dropbox on the.Dropbox (Local Mode) section.
3. Sign in to Dropbox account.
4. Tap Allow button.
5. Tap Ok button.
