# Start The Apache Server

1. Open Google Play Store.
2. Download AWebServer app on your Android device by click 'Install' button.
3. After your download and installation has finished. Open the **AWebServer** app.
4. If you have not purchased AWebServer, tap 'Continue to app' in the top right corner. 
5. tap 'Maybe later'. If you want to buy later.
6. In the first tab (Web Server). Click 'Start' button on the 'Service' row.
7. After the 'Start' button pressed. Look at the Address row. Remember and take a note for the IP Address, in this example http://192.168.0.2:8080.
8. Open a web browser, for example, Opera browser. Type your IP Address in a Browser Address box, in this example is 'http://192.168.0.2:8080' without single quotes.
